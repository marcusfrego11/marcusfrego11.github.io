Instruments

Epiphone Les Paul Classic (2021)

Honeyburst

Acquired: ???

My very first guitar. Despite its abuse (note the nickel-sized chip in the body), it remains a favourite guitar of mine. It is a versatile instrument that has served me well playing in Jazz Band as well as providing authentic tones for the 70s-90s rock I often find myself playing.

Modifications & Specifications

12/2025: Upgraded stock quick-connector wiring harness to a ToneShaper wiring kit with push-pull coil split pots.

08/2026: Switched knobs to goldtops for the rhythm controls and two-tone knobs for the treble controls, mirroring that of Alex Lifeson's "Honey" Les Paul. While solely aesthetic, it is one of my favorite visual touches of the guitar.

Marshall JCM800 Studio Classic

Acquired: 04/2026

The classic 80s Marshall monster in a small but mighty package. Despite having only 20 watts and a 10-inch speaker, this thing gets LOUD. Modern features such as an effects loop and digital interface output make this a highly versatile and powerful amplifier.

Modifications & Setup

No modifications have been made to this amp (yet). One I have in mind is a Hot Mod V2, a plug-in module that can be used to increase gain and sustain in many tube amps. I may also upgrade its speaker to a more premium 10-inch speaker or modify its housing to fit a 12-inch.

Pedals

Dunlop GCB-95 Cry Baby Wah (1988)

Acquired: 05/2026

Info about the Cry Baby Wah

Modifications & Setup

By far my most overhauled pedal. While it began life as a late-80s unit, it took less than an hour in my possesion to recieve significant modifications.

05/2026: Upgraded its inductor to a Jim Dunlop Red Fasel and changed capacitor and resistor values to mirror vintage Vox units.

09/2026: Removed stock rubber stoppers for greater physical sweep of the pedal and a more smooth motion.
